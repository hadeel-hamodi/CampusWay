# Screenshots

Screenshots of CampusWay taken from commit `521863b` (`main`, 6 October 2026). They are used throughout the documentation and can be reused in reports and presentations.

**How they were taken:** headless Chromium, desktop at 1440 × 900 and phone at 390 × 844 (2× pixel density), with the service worker disabled. They were captured **without internet access**, so the OpenStreetMap street tiles are missing and the campus map shows building outlines, markers and routes on a plain background. This is also how the app looks offline. To get versions with the street map, take the same screens from the live app.
